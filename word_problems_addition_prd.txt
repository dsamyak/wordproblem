1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This document defines product requirements for the "Word Problems Using Addition"
interactive lesson module, delivered as part of Intellia SG's Grade 1 Math program
(Lesson 2.5). The module targets Singapore Primary 1 students aged 6–7 and covers
addition word problems within 20 and 100 using a gamified, simulation-based learning
model fully aligned with the Singapore Mathematics Framework (MOE).

The product is a standalone web page hosted at:
https://intelliasg.com/courses/grade-1-math/lessons/word-problems-using-addition/

Built using React (JSX), it is designed to match the visual and UX architecture of the
existing Intellia SG lesson at https://readingnumbers.vercel.app/ — featuring the same
section layout, mascot system, progress map, simulation stations, practice phase, and
challenge phase structure established in Lesson 1.2.

2. PRODUCT VISION & GOALS

Vision
------
To make addition word problem-solving joyful, intuitive, and effective for 6–7 year old
Singapore students by combining animated story simulations, visual number models
(part-whole, number bond, number line), and adaptive gamified practice that rewards
effort and celebrates mastery.

Goals
-----
Goal                          | Metric
------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------
Learning Completion           | ≥85% of students complete the full learning journey
Practice Engagement           | ≥90% attempt at least 10 practice questions
Score Achievement             | Average quiz score ≥75% on first attempt
Session Duration              | Average engagement time ≥15 minutes per session
Curriculum Alignment          | 100% aligned to Singapore MOE Primary 1 syllabus
Question Variety              | 100 unique randomised word problem questions

Primary: Grade 1 Students (Age 6–7, Singapore)
- Limited independent reading ability — questions must use simple vocabulary and visuals
- Require concrete-pictorial-abstract (CPA) progression to grasp word problems
- Short attention spans — need frequent rewards, animations, and variety
- Learn through narrative and play — story-based gamification is essential
- Singapore cultural context — local names, settings, and familiar objects

Secondary: Parents & Teachers
- Assign as in-class activity, homework, or revision tool
- Expect strict alignment with MOE Primary 1 Mathematics syllabus
- Need to see visible progress tracking and topic coverage

           Singapore MOE Primary Mathematics Framework

Learning Objectives
-------------------
- Solve 1-step word problems involving addition within 20
- Solve 1-step word problems involving addition within 100
- Apply number bond model to represent and solve addition stories
- Apply part-whole model (bar model) to represent word problem structure
- Identify the "whole" and the "parts" in a word problem
- Use number line to count on and verify addition
- Read and interpret simple word problem sentences
- Write a number sentence (equation) from a word problem
- Understand "how many altogether", "how many in all", "total" as addition signals
- Use addition within 20: adding two 1-digit numbers
- Use addition within 100: adding 1-digit and 2-digit numbers

Singapore Syllabus Concepts Covered
-------------------------------------
- Addition within 20 (Section 8: Addition Within 20, Primary 1A — pages 90–103)
- Addition within 100 (Section 3: Addition Within 10, Primary 1A — pages 24–37)
- Word problems: "Addition Stories" (Primary 1A, pages 35–36)
- Number bonds as a visual model for part-whole relationships
- CPA progression: concrete objects → pictorial bar model → abstract equation
- Singapore heuristic: Draw a model / Write a number sentence

5.2 Intro Screen
-----------------
- Mascot (friendly monkey/animal character) welcomes the student
- Title card: "Word Problems Using Addition"
- Sub-heading: "Let's be a maths detective! 🔍"
- Animated preview of what they will learn (3-second auto-play)
- "Start Learning!" button → enters Phase 1

5.3 Progress Map (visible throughout all phases)
-------------------------------------------------
A horizontal journey map (matching readingnumbers.vercel.app style) with:
- Step 1: 📖 Learn (4 stations)
- Step 2: ✏️ Practice (10 questions)
- Step 3: 🏆 Challenge (100 questions, sets of 10)
- Current step glows / pulses
- Completed steps show green checkmarks

5.4 Learning Stations (Phase 1 — Simulation Phase)
----------------------------------------------------

Station | Topic                          | Simulation Type
--------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------------
  1     | What is a Word Problem?        | Animated story: characters + objects appear,
        |                                | mascot narrates, number sentence builds live
  2     | Addition Stories (Within 20)   | Interactive story scene: drag objects to
        |                                | combine groups, number bond fills in
  3     | Part-Whole Bar Model           | Build a bar model by dragging "part" blocks
        |                                | into the bar; equation auto-generates
  4     | Number Line Count On           | Animated number line: student places a frog
        |                                | that jumps to solve the addition

Station 1 Detail — "Story Scene" Simulation:
  • An animated classroom or playground scene plays
  • E.g.: "Mia has 4 🍎 apples. Ben gives her 3 more 🍎. How many does Mia have now?"
  • Objects animate on screen — 4 apples appear, then 3 more fly in and join
  • A number bond diagram fills: [4] + [3] = [?]
  • Student clicks the answer bubble
  • Mascot cheers: "That's right! 4 + 3 = 7! 🎉"

Station 2 Detail — "Drag and Combine" Simulation:
  • Student drags objects (fruits, animals, toys — Singapore-context items) from two
    groups into a combined circle
  • Counter shows live addition as objects are dragged
  • Covers within-20 numbers
  • Number bond visual updates dynamically

Station 3 Detail — "Build the Bar Model" Simulation:
  • Word problem text displayed (simple sentences, ≤12 words)
  • Student drags labelled blocks to form the part-whole bar model:
    [Part A] [Part B] = [Whole]
  • Mascot labels: "This is a PART. This is another PART. Together they make the WHOLE!"
  • Equation auto-generates below: "__ + __ = __"

Station 4 Detail — "Frog Jump Number Line" Simulation:
  • A number line 0–20 is shown
  • Student sees: "Start at 6. Jump 5 more. Where do you land?"
  • A cartoon frog sits at the starting number
  • Student taps the number line to place jumps (or uses + button)
  • Frog animates jumping each step, lands on answer
  • "6 + 5 = 11! Great jumping! 🐸"

5.5 Practice Phase (Phase 2 — 10 Guided Questions)
----------------------------------------------------
- 10 questions drawn from the question bank (scaffolded — simpler first)
- Each question includes a visual aid (picture, bar model scaffold, or number bond outline)
- Hint system: 2 hints per question before answer is revealed
  - Hint 1: Visual cue (highlight key words in the problem)
  - Hint 2: Bar model scaffold appears with one value filled
- Feedback: Immediate (correct = celebration, wrong = gentle retry)
- No timer — encouragement-first approach
- Score tracked but not shown until end of practice phase

5.6 Challenge Phase (Phase 3 — 100 Questions, Sets of 10)
-----------------------------------------------------------
- 100 randomly ordered word problems from the full question bank
- Displayed in groups of 10 (10 rounds)
- After each round of 10: star rating shown (1–3 stars), XP awarded, next round unlocked
- Mascot reacts to streak performance
- Students can exit and resume within 24 hours (localStorage persistence)

7. GAMIFICATION DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

7.1 Reward System
------------------
Stars (⭐): Earned per round of 10 questions
  - 9–10 correct = 3 stars ⭐⭐⭐
  - 7–8 correct  = 2 stars ⭐⭐
  - 5–6 correct  = 1 star  ⭐
  - 0–4 correct  = 0 stars (retry encouraged)

XP Points:
  - 10 XP: correct on first attempt
  - 7 XP:  correct on second attempt (no hints)
  - 5 XP:  correct after using a hint
  - Streak Bonus: +5 XP if streak ≥ 5

Badges (Unlockable):
  🏅 "Story Starter"    — Complete all 4 Learning Stations
  🥈 "Problem Solver"   — Complete 10 Practice questions
  🥇 "Addition Ace"     — Score 80%+ on Challenge phase
  💎 "Model Master"     — Use bar model correctly in 10+ questions
  🔥 "Hot Streak!"      — 10 consecutive correct answers
  🌟 "Perfect 100"      — Score 100/100 on Challenge phase
  🐸 "Number Line Hero" — Complete all number line simulations

Streak Counter:
  - Consecutive correct answers shown with fire emoji 🔥
  - "3 in a row! You're on fire! 🔥"
  - Counter resets on wrong answer

Progress Bar:
  - Persistent horizontal bar across top of page
  - Fills as: Learn → Practice → Challenge
  - Colour transitions: grey → blue → gold

7.2 Feedback Mechanics
-----------------------
Correct Answer:
  ✅ Bright celebratory animation (stars burst, confetti)
  ✅ Mascot jumps and cheers
  ✅ Sound: ascending tone / chime
  ✅ Message: "Amazing! 🎉", "That's it!", "You're a maths star! ⭐"

Wrong Answer:
  ❌ Gentle wobble animation (no harsh red flash)
  ❌ Mascot looks thoughtful ("Hmm, let's try again!")
  ❌ Sound: soft low tone
  ❌ Hint 1 appears immediately (keyword highlight in problem)
  ❌ Hint 2 appears on second wrong: bar model scaffold shown
  ❌ After 2 wrong: answer revealed with step-by-step explanation

No negative scoring — encouragement-first, mistake-tolerant approach.
Max 2 hints per question. Explanation always shown on 3rd attempt.

7.3 Mascot Character
---------------------
- Same monkey mascot as the Targeting Mathematics Primary 1A textbook theme
- Moods: idle (neutral), thinking (question), happy (correct), celebrating (badge unlock)
- Appears in corner throughout — gives tips, encouragement, and explanations
- Speaks simple narration via Web Speech API

9.2 Question Bank Constraints
-------------------------------
- Minimum 10 questions per question type (10 types = 100 total)
- All answers are whole positive numbers (no negative results)
- All addition sentences solvable without regrouping for levels 1–3,
  with optional regrouping for level 4–5 difficulty
- Singapore context names appear in ≥80% of questions
- No question repeats within a single session
- Word problems use vocabulary from the Singapore Primary 1 word list
- Maximum sentence length: 25 words per question

9.3 Audio Requirements
-----------------------
- Question read-aloud (Web Speech API, en-SG locale, rate 0.85)
- Celebration audio cues (ascending tones)
- Incorrect audio cue (soft descending tone)
- Badge unlock fanfare (3-note sequence)
- Background ambient music (optional, off by default, toggleable 🔇/🔊)
- All narration text paused/resumable
